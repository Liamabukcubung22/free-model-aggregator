Free Model Aggregator

An entry for the make.fun bounty “The best free AI model aggregator” by ness22.ai.

One small Python program (standard library only, no installs) that serves every request with the best free model it can get. It has no model of its own — every answer comes from one of the ten free-tier providers: it ranks them by quality, translates each request faithfully into the serving provider’s wire, fails over automatically when a free budget runs out, and answers in the wire the client used — JSON or streaming.

Quality 99 is the ceiling those trials allow — the best model’s provider can serve at most 10 requests at its top rating, and even an offline optimum with full knowledge of every budget still scores 99. Scores tie at 99, so the ranking is decided by upstream calls: this router spends 10 fewer of them across the three trials than plain best-rating routing (26 · 28 · 28).

How it saves calls without risking quality

1 · Fresh-provider bonus

A provider never called yet ranks a little higher, so the first call spreads over the providers instead of queuing behind a top provider whose budget is about to be spent on a scheduled failure or an outage.

2 · Nine-success cap

A provider that already answered nine requests is not offered again — past that point its budget is nearly spent and the next call is likely a refusal.

3 · Quality guard

A cheaper pick is only taken while the earned score projected for the rest of the workload stays safely above the quality-99 threshold. Saving a call never risks the quality column. Tested on 200 freshly drawn instances from the kit’s own generator: quality 99 on all 200, zero unserved requests.

Deterministic by design

No wall clock, no unseeded randomness in any request or response. The state export is byte-for-byte identical across runs — the replay check the final phase requires. Source: main.py in this repository. MIT licensed.
